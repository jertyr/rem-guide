Remodelers Guide: PPE and Safety Equipment Checklist

Personal Protective Equipment

  • ☐ Safety glasses (Z87) 1926.102
  • ☐ Hard hat (ANSI Z89.1) 1926.100
  • ☐ Hearing protection (plugs or muffs) 1926.101
  • ☐ Dust masks (N95) 1910.134
  • ☐ Respirator (half or full face, P100 cartridges) 1910.134
  • ☐ Work gloves (cut and chemical) 1926.95
  • ☐ Safety boots (ASTM F2413) 1926.96
  • ☐ High-visibility vest 1926.201

Training / Certifications Current

  • ☐ First aid / CPR certificate 1926.50(c)
  • ☐ Respirator medical eval + fit test 1910.134
  • ☐ Silica awareness training 1926.1153(i)
  • ☐ Ladder safety training 1926.1060
  • ☐ Competent person / safety coordinator named 1926.20(b)

Site Equipment

  • ☐ First aid kit (stocked, weatherproof) 1926.50
  • ☐ Fire extinguisher, 2A min within 100 ft 1926.150(c)
  • ☐ Fire extinguisher, 10B within 50 ft of flammables 1926.150(c)
  • ☐ GFCI cords / GFCI temp panel 1926.404(b)(1)
  • ☐ Emergency contacts posted on site 1926.50(f)
  • ☐ Electrical pen (non-contact voltage tester) 1926.416

Documentation Available (electronic copies)

  • ☐ SDS sheets for all chemicals on site 1910.1200
  • ☐ Written safety plan / accident prevention program 1926.20(b)
  • ☐ Emergency action plan 1926.35
  • ☐ Silica exposure control plan 1926.1153(g)
  • ☐ Site safety inspection forms (blank + completed) 1926.20(b)(2)
