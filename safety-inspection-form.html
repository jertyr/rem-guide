PPE in Use

  • ☐ Eye protection (Z87) worn where required 1926.102
  • ☐ Hard hats worn where overhead/impact hazard 1926.100
  • ☐ Hearing protection in high-noise tasks 1926.101
  • ☐ Respirators / dust masks used for dust & silica 1910.134
  • ☐ Foot & hand protection appropriate to task 1926.95

Housekeeping & Access

  • ☐ Walkways clear, debris and trip hazards removed 1926.25
  • ☐ Protruding nails bent or removed 1926.25(a)
  • ☐ Materials stacked and secured 1926.250
  • ☐ Adequate lighting in work areas 1926.56

Ladders & Fall Protection

  • ☐ Ladders set at 4:1, extend 3 ft above landing 1926.1053
  • ☐ Ladders sound, no damage, right type 1926.1053(b)
  • ☐ Fall protection at 6 ft or more 1926.501
  • ☐ Floor / roof openings guarded or covered 1926.501(b)(4)

Electrical

  • ☐ GFCI protection on all temp cords / outlets 1926.404(b)(1)
  • ☐ Cords intact, no damaged insulation, grounded 1926.405
  • ☐ Panels closed, no exposed energized parts 1926.403
  • ☐ Circuits verified de-energized before work 1926.416

Fire & Emergency

  • ☐ Fire extinguisher present, charged, within 100 ft 1926.150(c)
  • ☐ Flammables stored properly, 10B unit within 50 ft 1926.152
  • ☐ First aid kit stocked and accessible 1926.50
  • ☐ Emergency contacts posted on site 1926.50(f)

Hazard Communication & Dust

  • ☐ SDS accessible for chemicals on site 1910.1200
  • ☐ Containers labeled 1910.1200(f)
  • ☐ Silica dust controls in use (water / vacuum) 1926.1153
  • ☐ Exposure control plan on site 1926.1153(g)
