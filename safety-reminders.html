Remodelers Guide: Safety Reminders

At-a-glance jobsite reminders. Every line traces back to OSHA, so it holds up if you say it to an inspector. Keep this with your safety gear.

PPE

  • Z87 glasses on for cutting, grinding, and fastening. (1926.102)
  • Hard hat on whenever work is happening overhead. (1926.100)
  • If you have to shout at arm's length, ears in. (1926.101)
  • N95 for dust. P100 for silica, lead, and mold. (1910.134)
  • Cut gloves for blades and sheet goods; boots on. (1926.95)
  • Hi-vis vest near traffic and moving equipment. (1926.201)

Ladders

  • 4 in 1: one foot out for every four feet up. (1926.1053)
  • 3 feet above the roofline or landing. (1926.1053(b)(1))
  • Tie the top off or have a handhold.
  • Stepladder: never the top cap or top step. (1926.1053(b)(13))
  • Face it, three points of contact, belt buckle between the rails.

Falls & Holes

  • 6 feet up: guardrail, harness, or net. (1926.501(b)(1))
  • Cover and mark every hole in the floor or roof. (1926.501(b)(4))

Electrical

  • GFCI on every cord and temp outlet. (1926.404(b)(1))
  • Test it dead before you touch it. (1926.416)
  • Damaged cord, trash it. (1926.405)

Fire & First Aid

  • Charged extinguisher within 100 feet. (1926.150(c))
  • 10B unit within 50 feet of fuels. (1926.150(c))
  • Know the kit, the hospital, and the posted numbers. (1926.50)

Housekeeping & Dust

  • Bend or pull every nail. (1926.25(a))
  • Clear walkways; stack and secure materials. (1926.250)
  • Wet it or vac it. Never dry-cut silica. (1926.1153)

Before You Start

  • Know the day's hazards and who the competent person is. (1926.20(b))
  • SDS, safety plan, and emergency contacts on hand. (1910.1200)
