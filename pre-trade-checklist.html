Pre-Trade Checklist

What do the trades need so that they can do their best work?

General Preparation

  • ☐ Clean site
  • ☐ Updated plan for subs
  • ☐ Confirm scope of work with each trade
  • ☐ Locations marked on walls or prints
  • ☐ Check tub/shower rough openings fit units
  • ☐ Confirm banks of windows are level and square
  • ☐ Check door openings for size and plumb
  • ☐ Remind trades to schedule their inspections

Get Necessary Materials on Site

  • ☐ Shower valves and trim kits
  • ☐ Tubs and shower bases
  • ☐ Bath fans
  • ☐ Range hood
  • ☐ Appliance spec sheets
  • ☐ Cabinet drawings
  • ☐ Lighting fixtures / recessed housings
  • ☐ Special-order electrical (EV charger, generator interlock, etc.)

Kitchen

  • ☐ Printed cabinet drawings - elevations on walls
  • ☐ Cabinets laid out
  • ☐ Printed appliance specs
  • ☐ Appliances laid out with specs on site

Plumbing

  • ☐ Printed plumbing specs
  • ☐ Lay out bathrooms
  • ☐ Shower – Valve, elbow, head, handshower, rainfall, body sprays, etc.
  • ☐ Baseboard (toilet shut off location)
  • ☐ Elevation for vanity – Floating, thickened top, splash height

Electrical

  • ☐ Printed electrical specs
  • ☐ Lighting and outlets laid out
  • ☐ Bath fan locations

Doors and Finishes

  • ☐ Door sizes and swings
  • ☐ Mark floor finishes on site

Resources

  • Trade Inspection Checklist: walk-through after trades finish rough-in, before calling for inspections
