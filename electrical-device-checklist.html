Electrical Device Checklist

Everything in a home that may need power. Print this and walk each room before electrical rough to make sure nothing gets missed.

Kitchen

  • Refrigerator
  • Range / cooktop / wall oven
  • Microwave
  • Dishwasher
  • Range hood
  • Garbage disposal
  • Undercabinet lighting
  • In-cabinet lighting
  • Island pop-up outlet
  • Charging drawer / station
  • Coffee maker cabinet outlet
  • Stand mixer outlet
  • Under-sink motion light
  • Instant hot water dispenser
  • Counter receptacles per code

Bathrooms

  • Exhaust fan / light combo
  • Vanity light
  • Heated floor
  • Heated towel bar
  • Toe-kick heater
  • Lighted mirror
  • Bidet toilet seat
  • Electric toothbrush / razor outlet
  • GFCI outlets

Living / Family Room

  • TV prep
  • Soundbar / surround speaker wiring
  • Lamp outlets
  • Fireplace blower / electric fireplace
  • Sconce lights
  • Floor outlet

Bedrooms

  • Nightstand outlets (USB)
  • TV prep
  • Closet light
  • Ceiling fan / light combo
  • Sconce lights

Home Office

  • Desk outlets (power + USB + data)
  • Printer outlet
  • Data drops

Laundry

  • Washer
  • Dryer (electric 240V or gas 120V)
  • Iron / steamer outlet
  • Overhead lighting

Utility / Mechanical

  • Water heater
  • Furnace / boiler
  • Sump pump
  • Radon fan
  • Chest freezer
  • Spare refrigerator
  • Dehumidifier
  • Sump battery backup

Garage

  • Garage door opener(s)
  • EV charger
  • Workbench outlets
  • Chest freezer / spare fridge
  • Overhead lighting
  • Motion lights

Gym / Exercise Room

  • Treadmill / equipment outlet
  • TV prep
  • Sound system

Exterior

  • Exterior outlets (GFCI)
  • Exterior door lights
  • Landscape lighting transformer
  • Security cameras
  • Doorbell
  • Holiday lighting outlet (roofline / soffits)
  • Pool / hot tub
  • Grill area outlet
  • Irrigation controller

Whole-House

  • Smoke detectors (interconnected)
  • CO detectors
  • Internet router / network equipment
  • Whole-house surge protector
  • Generator interlock / transfer switch
  • Doorbell transformer
  • Central vacuum outlets
  • Battery vacuum charger mount
  • Stairway lighting (3-way switches)
