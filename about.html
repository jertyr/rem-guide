Welcome

The Remodelers Guide is a notebook of accumulated knowledge that I've put together. Some of this information was taught to me, and a lot of it was learned the hard way by running head first into a problem and needing to solve it to move on to the next step.

It is meant to aid builders and remodelers to make us more effective. To remind us of what might be important for the next step in a complex build process, and to offer clients a glimpse of the decisions they may be asked to make in order to complete a project.

This was written from a Midwestern point of view because that is where I work. Please reach out if you want to offer a suggestion.

Lets all build better together!

-Jerry Tyrrell
