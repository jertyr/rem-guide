Jobsite Protection Checklist

Process

  • ☐ Remove pictures from walls
  • ☐ Empty cabinets, fridges, and dishwashers
  • ☐ Move furniture out
  • ☐ Remove rugs and floor coverings
  • ☐ Mark any items you want saved
  • ☐ Cover door thresholds with tacky tape
  • ☐ Cover high traffic floors
    • ☐ Tape field seams with white tape
    • ☐ Tape edges with blue frog tape
    • ☐ Top with Masonite if needed
  • ☐ Cover hard surfaces
  • ☐ Drape furnishings with plastic
  • ☐ Install dust walls
  • ☐ Install magnet doors
  • ☐ Set up negative air if needed
  • ☐ Cover drains
  • ☐ Seal nearby doors and ducts
  • ☐ Cover new material

Materials / Tools Needed

  • ☐ Blue and white painters tape
  • ☐ Tacky tape
  • ☐ Painters plastic (light duty), 1 mil (standard), 3 mil (lead demo walls), 6 mil (lead demo floors)
  • ☐ Zip poles
  • ☐ Poly hanging tape
  • ☐ Magnet or zipper doors
  • ☐ Stapler
  • ☐ Floor protection (Albert Floorotex, reinforced builders paper)
  • ☐ Carpet shield (never apply this to wood floors or trim)
  • ☐ Masonite
  • ☐ Drop cloths
  • ☐ More permanent fixtures, like temp walls and doors
  • ☐ Fans for exhause or air scrubbers for lead demo jobs
  • ☐ Lay flat duct
