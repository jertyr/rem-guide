Demolition Checklist

Use this checklist to protect finishes, control dust, and leave the site safe at the end of each day. This is not a complete list, it is meant to provide a guide so that work can proceed in order, check the full articles for more detail.

Site protection

  • ☐ Cover floors
  • ☐ Remove wall hangings
  • ☐ Protect railings, door sills, counters, and other finishes
  • ☐ Cover vents and turn off the furnace
  • ☐ Put up dust walls
  • ☐ Install air scrubber

Demolition

  • ☐ Remove items to be saved
  • ☐ Disconnect appliances
  • ☐ Remove faucets
  • ☐ Remove items that are garbage
  • ☐ Remove nails and screws from framing
  • ☐ Remove tack strips
  • ☐ Square off drywall patches
  • ☐ Remove floor staples
  • ☐ Remove wax rings
  • ☐ Cap bare wires and plumbing
  • ☐ Cover or barricade holes in the floor
  • ☐ Vacuum
  • ☐ Haul away

Before you leave

  • ☐ Turn furnace back on
  • ☐ Check pilot lights
  • ☐ Turn electrical circuits back on if needed
  • ☐ Don’t forget to lock up
