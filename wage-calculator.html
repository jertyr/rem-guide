Wage Calculator

Total compensation calculator. Enter wages and benefits to see the annualized package and the effective hourly rate with benefits included. Useful for costing an employee or showing a carpenter what their package is actually worth.

Assumes 8-hour days and the entered weekly hours for all 52 weeks; holiday and vacation pay is valued on top of wages. Employee health contributions are shown for reference and not added to total compensation. Payroll taxes, workers comp, and liability insurance are not included.
