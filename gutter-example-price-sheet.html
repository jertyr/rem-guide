General Notes

  • Trip charge: $1.00/mile for jobs 40–70 miles; $2.00/mile for projects ≥ 75 miles (e.g., 100 miles = $200).
  • Credit‑card payments incur a 4 % service fee.
  • Combination 5″/6″ jobs: additional $70 logistics charge.
  • Measurement equivalents: 1 miter = 3 ft; 1 elbow = 2 ft; 1 end cap = 1 ft; 1 outlet = 1 ft; minimum downspout length = 10 ft.
  • For gutter protection estimates, use total footage of gutter plus miters.
