Excavation Checklist

Materials / Tools Needed

  • ☐ Shovel
  • ☐ Post Hole Digger
  • ☐ Plywood (to cover holes)
  • ☐ Marking paint
  • ☐ String lines
  • ☐ Lumber for beater blocks
  • ☐ Stakes
  • ☐ Flags

Process

  • ☐ Call Miss Dig (or your local utility locator)
  • ☐ Mark out other known utilities
    • ☐ Wells
    • ☐ Drains
    • ☐ Sprinklers
  • ☐ Cover frozen ground
  • ☐ Mark the dig and overdig
  • ☐ Mark new drain locations and dry wells
  • ☐ Remove plants
  • ☐ Clear a path for equipment
  • ☐ Plan for spoils
  • ☐ Cover or barricade the area after
