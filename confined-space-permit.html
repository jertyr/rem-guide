Atmospheric Testing 1926.1204(e)

Test in order: oxygen, then flammable, then toxic. Retest as conditions change. Instrument: Calibrated:

Gas Acceptable range Reading 1 / time Reading 2 / time Reading 3 / time
Oxygen (O₂) 19.5% – 23.5%
Flammable (LEL) < 10% LEL
Carbon monoxide (CO) < 25 ppm
Hydrogen sulfide (H₂S) < 10 ppm
Other:

If any reading is out of range, do not enter. Mechanically ventilate or provide a supplied-air (airline) respirator, then retest before entry.
