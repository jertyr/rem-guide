Complete Framing Checklist

  • ☐ Finish all framing on plan
  • ☐ Install window bucks, jack studs, stringer stiffeners
  • ☐ Joist blocking
  • ☐ Drywall backing
  • ☐ Fur out walls and ceilings as needed
  • ☐ Blocking at cabinet runs (along uppers, and at 34")
  • ☐ Checking openings are within 1/8" of plumb
  • ☐ Check door openings are 83" clear height (or consult door sheet)
  • ☐ Layout electrical locations and add blocks for switches
  • ☐ Lay out plumbing locations
  • ☐ Tub or shower surrounds fit
  • ☐ Add blocks for shower glass
  • ☐ Add blocks for grab bars
  • ☐ Blocks for mirrors
  • ☐ Blocks for wall hung sinks and toilets
  • ☐ Blocking for floating vanities
  • ☐ Sheeting on pocket doors
  • ☐ Blocks for toilet rolls (18" AFF), towel bars, robe hooks (48" and 72" AFF)
  • ☐ Blocks for TVs
  • ☐ Blocking for wood ceilings and beams
  • ☐ Closet blocks
  • ☐ Blocking for wainscoting
  • ☐ Confirm hangers, hardware, and structural screws are in place
  • ☐ Banks of windows are aligned
  • ☐ Drywall wrapped windows are shimmed
  • ☐ Tiled areas are plumb and square
  • ☐ Fire block dropped ceilings
  • ☐ Fire block around raised floors
  • ☐ Organize lumber
  • ☐ Frame attic access
  • ☐ Handrail blocking
  • ☐ Take out trash
  • ☐ Sweep

PM Tasks

  • ☐ Lay out baseboard height (especially by toilets)
  • ☐ Lay out cabinets
  • ☐ Lay out hvac supplies (in walls)
  • ☐ Lay out electrical switching
  • ☐ Lay out door swings
