Printable Safety Signs

Each sign is one full landscape page. Print the whole set, or open the print dialog and choose just the page numbers you need. Set the printer to Landscape. Sleeve or laminate for weather.

Signal words and colors follow ANSI Z535 (DANGER red, WARNING orange, CAUTION yellow, NOTICE blue, green for safety and exits). The specific word chosen for each sign is my call; adjust any you would rate differently.

The gray notes under each sign show where to post it and the OSHA reference. They are screen-only and do not print.

Warning
Construction Area
Authorized Personnel Only

Post at the entry to the work area to control access and warn the public. (1926.200, 1926.201)

Notice
Hard Hat and Eye Protection Required Beyond This Point

Post at the entry to any PPE-required area. (1926.200(h), 1926.100, 1926.102)

Notice
Hard Hat Area

Post where overhead work or falling-object hazards exist. (1926.100)

Notice
Eye Protection Required

Post at cutting, grinding, and nailing areas. (1926.102)

Notice
Hearing Protection Required

Post where saws, routers, and other loud tools run. (1926.101, 1926.52)

Notice
Respirator Required

Post at silica, mud-sanding, and masonry-cutting areas. (1910.134, 1926.1153)

Danger
High Voltage
Keep Out. Authorized Personnel Only.

Post at panels, temporary power, and exposed conductors. (1926.200(b), 1926.403)

Danger
Open Hole
Fall Hazard. Keep Clear.

Post at floor openings, stair openings, and unguarded edges. (1926.501; DANGER for open holes is my call)

Warning
Silica Dust Hazard
Wear a Respirator. Wet-Cut or Vacuum.

Post where concrete, masonry, tile, or stone is cut or ground. (1926.1153, 1910.1200)

Danger
No Smoking
No Open Flames. Flammable Materials.

Post near fuel, solvents, and flammable storage. (1926.151(a)(3))

Caution
Overhead Work
Falling Objects. Watch Above.

Post below active overhead work. (1926.200(c))

First Aid Kit

Post at the first aid kit location. (1926.50)

Fire Extinguisher

Post at the extinguisher so it is visible from a distance. (1926.150)

Exit

Post at the designated emergency exit. (1926.200(g))
