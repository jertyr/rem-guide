Job Start & Demo Order Sheet

One page, Letter size. Fill it out once at job start: schedule the site services, then write quantities in the boxes and text a photo of the sheet or hand it to whoever loads the job. Print with margins set to none. Protection materials and the order they go in are on the Jobsite Protection Checklist; responsibilities are on the Job Start Checklist.
