Trade Inspection Checklist

Walk this list after trades finish rough-in and before calling for inspections. Catching issues now avoids failed inspections and delays.

All Trades

  • ☐ Nail plates installed on all pipes and wires within 1.25" of stud edge (NEC 300.4(A)(1))
  • ☐ Firestopping complete at all penetrations through fire-rated assemblies
  • ☐ Document blocking locations for future use (photos or markup on plans)
  • ☐ Take photos and videos of all rough-in before it gets covered
  • ☐ Each trade has scheduled their rough inspection
  • ☐ Sweep and clean site for next phase

HVAC / Mechanical

  • ☐ Gas lines capped and pressurized; gauge reading 10 psi for ≥15 minutes
  • ☐ Exhaust terminations ≥3 ft from windows/doors, ≥10 ft from intakes (IRC M1506.3)
  • ☐ Combustion air openings sized and installed for confined mechanical rooms (IRC G2407)
  • ☐ No return air from bathrooms or closets (IRC M1602.2)
  • ☐ Ducts strapped and supported per code
  • ☐ Condensate drains trapped and sloped
  • ☐ Roof/wall caps and flashings installed
  • ☐ Thermostat wiring roughed to correct locations
  • ☐ Ducts sealed and masticked at joints

Plumbing

  • ☐ DWV test caps in place; system ready for pressure test
  • ☐ Gas lines capped and pressurized with gauge visible
  • ☐ Shower pan flood test passed (before tile or backer)
  • ☐ Shutoff valves installed at each fixture
  • ☐ Valve depths verified against finished wall thickness (tile + backer)
  • ☐ Tubs and shower bases protected and undamaged
  • ☐ Cleanouts accessible
  • ☐ Vent pipes routed to planned roof penetrations
  • ☐ Drain slopes correct; no drains landing on joists or beams

Electrical

  • ☐ Confirm outlets and switches are at correct locations and heights
  • ☐ All circuits labeled at panel
  • ☐ Dedicated circuits wired per code (range, dishwasher, dryer, furnace, water heater, bath receptacles, kitchen small appliance)
  • ☐ Recessed light housings IC-rated where adjacent to insulation
  • ☐ Low-voltage runs complete (data, camera, speaker, doorbell)
  • ☐ Smoke and CO detector wiring in place (interconnected)
  • ☐ GFCI protection at all required locations (bath, kitchen, garage, exterior)
