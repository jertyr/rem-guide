HVAC / Mechanical Rough Install

This guide covers rough-in coordination for forced-air, hydronic (boiler + radiator and radiant-floor), mini-split, and high-velocity retrofit (Unico) systems, along with exhaust fans and appliance preparation. Scope includes layout, penetrations, clearances, pressure tests, and cross-trade hand-offs—not fabrication details.

Quick Reference

  • Pull mechanical and gas permits before start of rough
  • Collect BTU, CFM, and amp specs for every appliance (including utility entry locations)
  • Mark all terminations ≥3 ft from windows, ≥10 ft from intakes (IRC M1506.3)
  • No return air from baths or closets (IRC M1602.2)
  • Exhaust hoods >400 CFM require interlocked make-up air (IRC M1503.6)
  • Confirm combustion air in any confined mechanical room (IRC G2407)
  • Gas pressure test ≥10 psi for ≥15 minutes before inspection
  • Don't forget thermostat locations

Prerequisites

  • Mechanical and gas permits pulled
  • Appliance schedule created with BTU, CFM, amp draw for all equipment:
    • Gas: furnace, boiler, water heater, fireplace, range/oven, dryer, exterior grill, generator, pool heater, garage heater
    • Power: furnace blower, boiler pumps, mini-split indoor/outdoor units, ERV/HRV, dehumidifier, radiant actuators or pumps, range, hood, dryer, stove, oven, air conditioner, garage heater, bath fan
    • Water: boiler make-up, condensate, radiant supply/return
  • Thicken or fur out walls for duct runs where needed
  • All termination exit points marked with 3 ft / 10 ft clearances
  • Electrician coordinated for power to equipment and bath fan installation
  • Temporary heat plan in place (electric heaters or early-start furnace)
  • Subfloor removed in remodel situations where needed

Process

  • Layout
    • Walk job with trades
    • Confirm supply/return grille locations, split heads, bath fans, ERV ports
    • Verify boiler, outdoor unit, and radiant manifold locations
    • Plan for supplies to wash windows and doors
    • Confirm termination locations with clearance requirements
  • Core and Frame Penetrations
    • Drill or rotary-core all ducts, vents, and line-sets
    • Install siding blocks or terminations if needed before exterior finish
  • Install Ducts, Tubing, or Splits
    • No returns from bathrooms or closets (IRC M1602.2)
    • Strap and support per code requirements
  • Combustion Air
    • Determine if mechanical room is confined: volume < 50 cu ft per 1,000 BTU/hr of all appliances in the space (IRC G2407)
    • If confined, provide combustion air via openings or ducts sized per method used:
      • Same-story indoor air: two permanent openings into adjacent space—one within 12" of top, one within 12" of bottom—each ≥ 1 sq in per 1,000 BTU/hr (min 100 sq in each)
      • Different-story indoor air: permanent openings (e.g. louvered stair door) totaling ≥ 2 sq in per 1,000 BTU/hr
      • Outdoor air, single opening: ≥ 1 sq in per 3,000 BTU/hr, within 12" of ceiling
      • Outdoor air, two openings: vertical duct ≥ 1 sq in per 4,000 BTU/hr; horizontal duct ≥ 1 sq in per 2,000 BTU/hr
    • Use net free area for louvers: 75% for metal, 25% for wood
    • Insulate combustion air ducts to R-8 where passing through conditioned space
  • Combustion and Exhaust Terminations
    • Exhaust outlets ≥3 ft from windows/doors
    • Exhaust outlets ≥10 ft from mechanical intakes (or ≥3 ft above intake)
    • Reference IRC M1506.3 for specific clearances
  • Fresh Air and ERV Ducts
    • Provide dedicated 6" insulated duct for make-up/fresh air where ERV not used
    • Hoods >400 CFM require interlocked make-up air (IRC M1503.6)
  • Install Bath Fan and Hood Ducts
  • Install Dryer Exhaust
  • Install Roof/Wall Caps and Flashings
  • Install Appliance Gas Lines
  • Condensate and Dehumidifier Drains
    • Trap and slope ¼" per foot to pump or daylight
  • Seal, Mastic, and Check for Leaks

Inspections

  • Gas Pressure Test: 10 psi for ≥15 minutes, witnessed or with timestamped photo
  • Mechanical Rough: Duct sizes, strap spacing, vent/intake clearances, fresh air connection verified

Best Practices

  • Flag any hood >400 CFM during design review—make-up air is easy to overlook
  • Assign roof cap flashing responsibility (roofer vs. HVAC) before drilling penetrations
  • Note pre-existing condition of old radiant valves in contract; exercising during purge may reveal leaks (treat repair as change order)
  • Verify all termination clearances early—exhaust, dryer, bath fan, and dehumidifier outlets must be ≥3 ft from windows/doors and ≥10 ft from any mechanical intake unless ≥3 ft above that intake (IRC M1506.3)
  • Size combustion air openings during layout—retrofitting ducts into a finished mechanical room is difficult
  • Schedule electrician to pre-rough circuits where equipment needs power before HVAC rough

Client Communication

  • Confirm thermostat locations, grille style/color, and mini-split head positions early
  • Explain make-up air damper requirements and why large range hoods trigger extra ductwork
  • Advise that exercising old radiant valves during purge may reveal leaks; repairs not in base scope
  • Warn of temporary heat limitations and lack of cooling during rough phase
