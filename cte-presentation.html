Dexter Builders

We are a full-service design-build remodeling company based in southeast Michigan. Our work covers everything from additions and full renovations to roofing, masonry, excavation, and kitchen and bath supply. Every project is built by a team of skilled people across a wide range of trades and roles.

Our Companies

Four companies, one team

Dexter Builders operates as a family of four related companies that together cover the full range of construction and remodeling work.

Dexter Builders

Additions, kitchens, bathrooms, basements, carpentry, and design

Dexter Block

Masonry repairs and restoration, foundation work, bricklaying, cultured stone, flat work, pavers and hardscape

Dexter Roof

Roofs, siding, windows and doors

Mill Creek

Cabinet sales, countertops, tile, interior design
