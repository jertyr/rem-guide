Window and Door Install Checklist

  • ☐ Lumber (packout)
  • ☐ Flashing tape
  • ☐ Sealant
  • ☐ Shims
  • ☐ 1.75" Roofing nails
  • ☐ Low expansion spray foam (backer rod and sealant)
  • ☐ Air sealing tape
  • ☐ Interior trim
    • ☐ Extension jamb
    • ☐ Sill (aka Stool)
    • ☐ Casing
    • ☐ Apron
    • ☐ Shoe moulding or cove
    • ☐ Painters caulking
  • ☐ Exterior trim
    • ☐ Head flashing
    • ☐ Extension jamb
    • ☐ Sill
    • ☐ Casing
    • ☐ Apron
    • ☐ Exterior Sealant
  • ☐ Misc
    • ☐ Treated lumber for bucks
    • ☐ Trim screws
